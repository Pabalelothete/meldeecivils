Our Story of Excellence

Founded in 2020, Meldee Civil Works (Pty) Ltd has rapidly established itself as a trusted name in South Africa's civil engineering landscape. What began as a vision to deliver quality infrastructure has evolved into a comprehensive engineering solutions provider serving diverse industries across the nation.

Our journey is defined by a relentless commitment to excellence, safety, and innovation. From our headquarters in Kempton Park, Gauteng, we have successfully completed numerous projects that have transformed communities and contributed to South Africa's economic growth.

Led by our visionary directors Meldon Thete and Mikateko Mathebula, our team brings together decades of combined experience in civil engineering, project management, and construction excellence. We are proud to be a 100% black-owned and youth-owned enterprise, contributing to South Africa's transformation agenda.

Our Vision & Purpose

Guided by clear objectives and unwavering commitment

Our Vision

To be South Africa's premier civil engineering company, recognized for innovative solutions, sustainable practices, and unwavering commitment to quality. We envision transforming communities through infrastructure that stands the test of time, creating lasting value for generations to come.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional civil engineering services that exceed client expectations, ensure safety at every step, and contribute positively to South Africa's economic growth. We are committed to building relationships as strong as the structures we create, fostering trust through transparency and results.

Our Purpose

To build infrastructure that connects communities, enables economic growth, and improves quality of life. We believe every project is an opportunity to make a positive impact, and we approach each one with dedication, expertise, and a commitment to excellence that defines who we are.
